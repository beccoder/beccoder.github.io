About me

I'm a backend engineer and write mostly Go. Most of my work has been in fintech and other high-load systems where uptime, correctness, and performance matter: payment processing, real-time sync with banks, and integrations with external services.

Right now I work on Incard.uz, an auto-payment platform connected to more than 200 financial institutions. Before that I built services for a digital health platform on HL7 FHIR and for the Uzbekistan Football Association.

I pay close attention to data consistency and transaction correctness, because in payments a stale record turns into a wrong charge. I write down how my systems work so they stay maintainable after release, and I'm comfortable explaining technical trade-offs to people who aren't engineers.
